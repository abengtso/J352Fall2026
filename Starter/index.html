I LOVE MY PETS: RUE, CHEERIO AND TEDDY

I miss my them a lot and they are the best pets ever so this is their fan site!

-Rue is a female boxer-

She is a new addition to the family. We got her July 2026, so only a few months ago.

So far, we know that Rue likes strawberries, she has a LOT of energy, she doesn't like her crate and her fur makes her look like a mini tiger.

She also likes chasing you to try to bite your pants.

-Cheerio is a male king charles cavalier spaniel-

We have had Cheerio for almost seven years.

He was a menace when he was a baby, but now, he basically just sleeps all day besides his walks. Cheerio barks at anything that moves. He sits on a bench in the kitchen, staring out the window all day waiting for people to walk by so he can bark.

Cheerio loves cheese, following people around, going for car rides and of course, barking.

Cheerio hates strangers (especially DoorDashers), getting a haircut, being home alone and when the fan above the stove is on.

-Teddy is a male blue russian cat-

We have had him for about eight or nine years.

He loves his cat tree, when the printer prints paper, treats, cat toys and eating you hair.

Teddy hates being held, being pet for more than a minute, being put in his cat carrier (to get him to vet) and getting fed breakfast anytime after five a.m.

Teddy will sit in front of the cabinet that his treats are in and he will scratch at the door for forever until you give him a treat.

Honorable Mention: Lucy

-Lucy was a female boxer-

Lucy was my childhood dog we got when I was little, she sadly passed when I was in middle school.

Lucy was super smart, she liked to walk herself with her leash in her mouth and would calmly stay next to you. She would also play the "don't let the balloon touch the ground" game.
